DAILY WELLBEING

Small habits. Better wellbeing.

Looking after yourself does not always require a huge lifestyle change. Small consistent habits can make your daily routine healthier.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Keep water nearby and remember to drink regularly.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Give your body and mind enough time to rest.

🥗

Eat Regularly

Try not to regularly skip meals during busy study days.

🚶

Keep Moving

Walk, stretch or include simple physical activity.

📵

Take Screen Breaks

Give your eyes and mind some time away from screens.

👥

Stay Connected

Spend time with friends, family or people you trust.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WellNest FAQ

Is WellNest a medical service?

No. WellNest is an educational digital wellbeing resource. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.

Should I use WellNest instead of counselling?

No. The tools are intended for everyday wellbeing and self-care. Students who need professional support should seek help from qualified professionals.

Does WellNest store my mood?

No. The Mood Check is designed as a simple interactive check-in and does not require students to create an account or submit personal information.
