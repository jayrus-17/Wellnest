WHY WELLNEST?

Student life can be overwhelming.

Students may experience academic pressure, deadlines, examinations, lack of sleep, personal responsibilities and everyday stress. WellNest provides small, practical digital tools that encourage students to pause and take care of their wellbeing.

📚

Academic Pressure

Assignments, exams and deadlines can make student life stressful.

🧠

Mental Wellbeing

🌿

Simple Support

📵

👥

WHEN TO REACH OUT

You do not have to handle everything alone.

DIGITAL LITERACY

Think before you trust.

Online wellbeing information is not always accurate. Students should check important health information against reliable sources and qualified professionals.

🔎

Check the Source

Look for information from universities, government agencies, healthcare organisations and qualified professionals.

⚠️

Avoid Misinformation

Do not automatically believe health claims simply because they appear on social media.

🔐

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information with unknown websites or online services.
