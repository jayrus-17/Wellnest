DAILY WELLBEING

Small habits. Better wellbeing.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Keep water nearby and remember to drink regularly.

😴

Get Enough Sleep

Give your body and mind enough time to rest.

🥗

Eat Regularly

Try not to regularly skip meals during busy study days.

🚶

Keep Moving

Walk, stretch or include simple physical activity.

📵

Take Screen Breaks

Give your eyes and mind some time away from screens.

👥

Stay Connected

Spend time with friends, family or people you trust.
